HOW TO PLAY

Objective

  • Feed all three cats during the night.
  • Keep Gary out of the food as much as you can.
  • Avoid startling the cats and preserve their trust.
  • Earn Chow at dawn and spend it in PREP to improve the yard.
  • Survive nights that grow harder as Gary learns your tactics.

Game flow

  • PREP — buy upgrades and review what Gary has been up to.
  • DUSK — place and move bowls, barriers, and the sprinkler.
  • NIGHT — cats feed, Gary raids, and active defenses (and the hose) do the work.
  • DAWN / RESULTS — review the night, read the report, earn Chow.

Mouse

  • Drag — orbit the camera.
  • Scroll — zoom.
  • Right-drag — pan.
  • Click a bowl / barrier / sprinkler — select; drag it to move it (DUSK).
  • Click a cat — inspect its temperament (DUSK).
  • Pick a build tool, then click the lawn — place a defense (DUSK).
  • Hold with the garden hose — aim and spray (NIGHT).

Keyboard

1 Inspect2 Food bowl3 Barrier4 SprinklerH Garden hoseSpace Start night

Strategy

  • No single defense stays reliable forever — Gary learns repeated tactics.
  • Where you put the sprinkler matters more than what it sprinkles.
  • The hose is powerful, but it can frighten the cats.
  • Princess is especially sensitive to disturbances.
  • Watch Gary. He watches you — and he remembers.
  • Complementary defenses beat one-trick setups.
